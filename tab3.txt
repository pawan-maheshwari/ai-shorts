ANALYST DESK
research analysts · macro strategist · portfolio manager — shadow mode
At 09:30 IST the desk meets with only what is known by then — the first 15 minutes, earlier sessions and the Sentinel's pre-open notes. Each research analyst gives a view on one stock for the rest of the day (long, short or no view) with a thesis and what would prove it wrong; the portfolio manager turns the views, the market regime and the armed strategy specialists into a plan. Every view is scored like a real desk scores its analysts: the stock's move from 09:30 to 15:15 in the called direction, minus 0.1% costs. An LLM (Claude) writes the views when its API key is set; a rule-based analyst runs alongside as the benchmark it has to beat. Shadow mode: the desk never changes a live trade — its record decides whether it should.
