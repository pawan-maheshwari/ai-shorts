IoT Finger Pulse Scan

Place your fingertip flat over the rear camera and flash. The flash lights up your fingertip and the camera reads tiny blood-flow changes (photoplethysmography — the same optical sensing used in smartwatches) to measure your pulse and build your live vitals panel.

--
BPM
Sensor idle — tap Start Pulse Scan
  1. 1 Tap Start Pulse Scan and allow camera access.
  2. 2 Gently cover the rear camera + flash with either index fingertip. The screen waveform turns red-pink when contact is good.
  3. 3 Hold still for ~20 seconds. Your BPM and vitals appear automatically and merge with your BMI metrics below.

Works best on mobile with a rear flash (torch mode is switched on automatically when supported). On devices without a flash, use bright light behind your finger.

Apple Health & Watch Sync

One tap on your iPhone pulls your latest weight (from a smart scale or Health) and heart rate (from Apple Watch) straight into VitalMetrics — no backend, no login. Powered by an iOS Shortcut that opens this page with your data in the link.

One-Time Shortcut Setup (iPhone)
  1. 1 Open the Shortcuts app → tap + to create a new shortcut named "Sync VitalMetrics".
  2. 2 Add action Find Health Samples → Type: Weight, Sort by: Start Date (Latest First), Limit: 1.
  3. 3 Add a second Find Health Samples → Type: Heart Rate, same sorting, Limit: 1.
  4. 4 Add Open URLs and paste the template below, replacing the bracketed parts with the two Health Sample magic variables.
  5. 5 Run it (or add to Home Screen / ask Siri). VitalMetrics opens with everything pre-filled and recalculated. ✅
Your Sync URL Template
https://your-site/vitalmetrics.html?weight=[Weight]&hr=[Heart Rate]&source=health

Supported parameters: weight, height, hr, age, gender (male/female), unit (metric/imperial). Weight from a smart scale synced to Apple Health flows through automatically. Data stays on-device — the URL is processed locally in your browser.
