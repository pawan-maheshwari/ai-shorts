🧠 MODEL LAB
which brain gives the Coach the best win odds
Every brain scores each signal with a win probability before the result is known; models are retrained as sessions are added and only ever tested on days they had not seen. All signals won 55%. Taking only the top 30% by rule table (current coach) would have won 74% (+0.35R per trade vs +0.12R for all). Models learn from 2,281 practice signals (both directions, lower volume bar) so they have enough examples; they are judged on the signals the engine really gives.

● = brain in use · 103 real signals tested. Skill (AUC) is the chance a winner was scored above a loser: 0.50 is guessing, 0.55–0.60 is useful, above 0.65 is rare on real markets. A higher win rate only matters if R per trade rises with it.

How selective?

Each row keeps only the signals this brain liked most. “Win-odds bar” is the value to put in “Skip if win odds below” (Fine-tune → Coach) to get that row. If “Model said” and “Actually won” are far apart, trust the ranking but not the percentage.
