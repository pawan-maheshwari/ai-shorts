🔭 Scout is the strategy-research agent. The other agents can only filter, size or exit the signals they are given; Scout looks for better signals. Every day it tests thousands of intraday strategies — 9 setup families with different timings, sides, market and volume filters, stop sizes and targets — using the same fills and costs as the live engine. It picks candidates on the older sessions and keeps only those that stayed profitable on the newest sessions, which played no part in choosing them. A strategy only goes live after a reviewer approves it.
